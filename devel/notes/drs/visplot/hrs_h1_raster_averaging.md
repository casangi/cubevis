# HRS H1: scalar / vector averaging in raster cells

*2026-10-03; rebased 2026-10-05 onto `main` at `7f6e2f5`. Plan:
`hrs_visplot_plan.md` (milestone H1).*

## What was wrong

`_raster_2d()` in both backends reduced every quantity except Z-Score as
`mean(per-sample quantity)`. For Phase that is the arithmetic mean of
wrapped angles in degrees, which is not a phase: samples at +179 and -179
degrees (2 degrees apart) averaged to 0 instead of 180. Any Phase raster
cell covering samples on both sides of the wrap was wrong, most visibly on
Time x Baseline and Frequency x Baseline, where a whole dimension is
averaged. Amplitude was scalar-averaged only, so it could not show loss of
coherence.

## Definitions

| Mode | Amplitude | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| `scalar` (default) | mean of abs(V): unchanged from before | circular mean: direction of the mean unit phasor, each sample weighted equally |
| `vector` | abs of mean(V) | angle of mean(V): amplitude weighted |

Real and Imaginary are linear, so they are the same in both modes. Flag
fraction and Z-Score ignore the mode. Flagged samples and NaN padding are
excluded; a cell with no usable sample is NaN. When the reduced dimensions
all have size one (single-baseline waterfall) the per-sample value is
shown, as before.

## Decisions

- **Default is `scalar`**, so Amplitude rasters are numerically unchanged
  and nothing that depends on them moves. Phase is corrected in both
  modes: the old behaviour was a bug and is not offered as a mode.
- AIPS and plotms average vectorially by default. For HRS commissioning
  `vector` is probably the right default; switching is one line
  (`SelectionSpec.averaging` and the constructor default) and should be
  decided after looking at real VLBI-style data in both modes.
- The mode travels on `SelectionSpec` because that object already reaches
  the backend on every call, locally and through the remote worker, so no
  signatures in the reader protocol or wire layer changed. Side effect:
  it is part of the frame-cache fingerprint, so changing the mode costs
  one scatter re-read even though scatter does not use it.

change cells near +/-180 degrees show spurious values near 0; after, they
do not. Then switch Averaging to Vector with quantity Amplitude: calibrated
data should barely change.

## Known remaining issue (H1b)

Averaging is now right inside the backend, but two later stages still treat
the Phase image as an ordinary scalar field:

1. the Level-1 Datashader `Canvas.raster()` resample, which averages agg
   cells into screen pixels when zoomed out;
2. linear interpolation when upsampling (`raster_interpolate="linear"`).

Either can blend +179 and -179 degree cells into values near 0. Stride
decimation (`_decimate_agg`) is unaffected: it picks cells, it does not
average them. The fix is to carry cos/sin (or real/imag) planes through the
resample and take the angle afterwards; it belongs with the phase waterfall
preset and cyclic colormap in H3.
