# Historical reference

`cubevis` grew out of the `casagui` package. After a trade study of
visualization technologies for CASA, `casagui` was designed and built
around [Bokeh](https://bokeh.org), with interactive clean as its first
application. The pages in this section are the design and implementation
documents from that period.

They are kept because the reasoning in them still explains much of how
`cubevis` is put together: the usage settings it must serve (headless,
terminal, notebook, application), the boundary between Python and the
browser, and the first interactive clean and image-cube components. They
are not maintained, and names, figures and plans in them may no longer
match `cubevis`. New documentation for `cubevis` is being written in the
user guide; for how `cubevis` is put together today, see the
[slide decks](../slides.md).
